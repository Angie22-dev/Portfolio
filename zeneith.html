An exploration of how dense enrollment data can be restructured into an approachable desktop and mobile experience.

THE CHALLENGE

The supplied Active Enrollments screen distributes policy details across a wide administrative table. Finding renewal dates, carriers, and contribution information means scanning multiple columns.

THE DESIGN RESPONSE

The proposed desktop design separates carriers into large panels. The mobile layout stacks the same renewal overview into a vertical sequence, allowing key information to be read without horizontal table navigation.

02 / ARTWORK

Explore the screens.

03 / DESIGN THINKING

Small decisions.
Big clarity.

01

Find the provider first

Carrier names and policy headings lead each panel, followed by important renewal dates.

02

Design for two contexts

The desktop view uses a split composition while the narrow mobile view creates a continuous, stacked reading flow.

03

Respect what remains unfinished

The concept focuses on renewal information. Original enrollment reports, approvals, and administrative controls would need dedicated flows in a working product.

04 / NEXT ITERATION

What I’d examine next.

A next iteration would check small-text readability, color contrast, the clarity of insurance terminology, and where the original administrative actions belong. These are proposed checks, not claims of completed testing.
