THE CHALLENGE

A parking experience has to communicate where the user will park, what the stay may cost, and when it begins and ends—without losing the primary action.

THE DESIGN RESPONSE

This screen brings the garage identity and pricing into a bright yellow hero area, then places arrival and departure controls above a distinctive Slide to Park action.

02 / ARTWORK

Explore the screens.

Original Adobe XD-era artwork presented without recoloring or redesign. Select an image to see it larger.

03 / DESIGN THINKING

Small decisions.
Big clarity.

01

Make place and price visible

The garage name, vehicle illustration, and two rate options dominate the upper portion of the screen.

02

Make scheduling explicit

Today, Tomorrow, and Schedule choices sit near the arrival-time display; departure information is treated separately.

03

Give the primary action a clear destination

The Slide to Park control is anchored at the bottom of the composition. A real prototype would need to define what occurs after activation.

04 / NEXT ITERATION

What I’d examine next.

The supplied artifact shows one screen, not a tested end-to-end journey. Additional states worth designing include price confirmation, invalid times, tracker permissions, and successful reservation feedback.
