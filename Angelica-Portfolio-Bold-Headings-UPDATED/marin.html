THE CHALLENGE

Employee benefits information includes eligibility rules, multiple providers, and important coverage details that can be difficult to read as one undifferentiated list.

THE DESIGN RESPONSE

The designs use a distinctive purple-to-blue visual language, prominent benefit titles, and repeated provider modules to separate different plans.

02 / ARTWORK

Explore the screens.

Original Adobe XD-era artwork presented without recoloring or redesign. Select an image to see it larger.

03 / DESIGN THINKING

Small decisions.
Big clarity.

01

Establish a visual identity

The benefits experience pairs saturated color with white typography and selected blue accents.

02

Group related benefits

Medical information appears alongside eligibility and additional benefits; the continuation covers dental, vision, life insurance, and an FSA.

03

Use repetition to support scanning

Provider headings, plan types, dates, and coverage details follow consistent content patterns.

04 / NEXT ITERATION

What I’d examine next.

Future refinement would focus on increasing smaller text sizes and contrast for accessibility, validating the labels, and defining navigation between benefit types. The screenshots are presented as original design artifacts.
