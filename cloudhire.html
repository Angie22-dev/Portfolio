THE CHALLENGE

Recruitment discovery can begin from different mental models: the technology needed, the job being hired, or the larger career discipline. The original project presentation emphasizes benefits more than the browsable interface.

THE DESIGN RESPONSE

The updated visual places the catalog at center stage, with repeated cards divided into skills, roles, and careers to make different paths into the information visible.
